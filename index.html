About Baruva Village

Baruva is a historic coastal village located in Sompeta Mandal, Srikakulam District, Andhra Pradesh. Once a famous seaport, today Baruva is known for its pristine coconut-lined beach, spiritual temples, friendly community, and rich maritime history.

Major Attractions

🏖️ Baruva Beach

Serene coastline with coconut trees, sunrise views, and peaceful vibes.

🛕 Sri Koti Lingeswara Temple

Ancient Lord Shiva temple attracting devotees from across the region.

🛕 Puri Jagannath Temple

A spiritual landmark reflecting Odisha–Andhra cultural harmony.

🛕 Kanaka Durga Matha

Powerful goddess temple with local rituals and festivals.

🗼 Baruva Lighthouse

Historic lighthouse offering panoramic views of the Bay of Bengal.

🐢 Tortoise Conservation Center

Protected breeding center promoting marine conservation.

The SS Chilka Shipwreck

In July 1917, the British-era cargo ship SS Chilka sank off Baruva Beach. Today, its wreck lies close to shore, marked by a memorial pillar and surrounded by vibrant marine life. Baruva’s shipwreck is now a unique marine tourism and diving attraction.

Culture & Lifestyle

Baruva celebrates traditional festivals, coastal cuisine, fishing life, and strong community values. The people are warm, welcoming, and deeply connected to nature and spirituality.
